Document Plagiarism Checker

Google search-based text originality check

Method, honestly: every sentence is quoted and searched through the googlesearch library on Google (TLD co.id, 10 results per query, small pause between queries). Hits on blocked domains — LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, Reddit and major marketplaces — are filtered out. The index is matched sentences ÷ total sentences × 100. A match means the exact sentence appeared in the first results for its quoted query; it is not a semantic similarity score.

What was searched

  1. Split. Your input (pasted text or extracted PDF text) is split into sentences on ". " after newlines are flattened to spaces.
  2. Quote. Each sentence becomes an exact-phrase query: "its words, inner quotes softened", sent to Google through googlesearch with tld="co.id", num=10, stop=10, pause=2.
  3. Filter. Results pointing at blocked domains are dropped: id.linkedin.com, linkedin.com, youtube.com, instagram.com, facebook.com, tokopedia.com, twitter.com, reddit.com, bukalapak.com, shopee.com, blibli.com.
  4. Score. (matched sentences ÷ total sentences) × 100 is the index shown in the gauge.

Honest limits: this is a web-presence check, not a teacher's judgement. Paraphrased or translated text will not match, a single sentence can appear in the top 10 for many reasons, and every query leaves this server toward Google — rate limits can slow or break a long scan.
