3. Common student misconceptions: Some common misconceptions related to the respiratory system might include:
   - Not understanding the role of each part of the respiratory system (nose, trachea, lungs, etc.) in breathing
   - Thinking that only the lungs exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide during respiration
   - Failing to grasp the concept of ventilation and its relationship to gas exchange
   - Incorrectly assuming that the size or shape of an organ within the respiratory system affects its function
