2. Real-world applications:
The provided SVG diagram is a simple representation used to explain biological concepts related to cell division, DNA replication, and genetics. It can be used in various educational settings such as online courses, presentations, and learning materials for students of all ages.

3. Common student misconceptions:
- Many students might assume that organisms create exact copies of themselves during reproduction or mitosis (cell division). However, it is essential to clarify that there are small random changes in the genetic material (DNA) that occur during these processes, a phenomenon known as mutations.
- Additionally, some students may misunderstand how traits are passed from parents to offspring and how this contributes to genetic diversity among organisms. It is crucial to explain the concepts of dominant and recessive genes, genetic inheritance patterns, and independent assortment in this context.

   - Encourage discussions or collaborative activities where students can share their findings, ask questions, and learn from each other's experiences, helping them build a deeper understanding of these complex biological processes.
