2. Real-world applications:
This educational diagram would be used in biology classes teaching human reproduction to help students understand the processes involved in conception, fertilization, and the development of an embryo into a fetus. It could also be useful in sex education programs or for self-study purposes.

3. Common student misconceptions:
   - Misconception 1: Believing that conception happens instantly when sperm meets an egg (ova). In reality, it takes several days for the sperm to fertilize the egg and for implantation to occur in the uterus.
   - Misconception 2: Thinking that pregnancy always occurs when having unprotected sexual intercourse. Factors such as ovulation timing, sperm count and mobility, cervical mucus, and age can affect the likelihood of conceiving.
   - Misconception 3: Assuming that every fertilized egg will result in a successful pregnancy. Many fertilized eggs do not implant or may be lost during early stages of development due to chromosomal abnormalities or other factors.
