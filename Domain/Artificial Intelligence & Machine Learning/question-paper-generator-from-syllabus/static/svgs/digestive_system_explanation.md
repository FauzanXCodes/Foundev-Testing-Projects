3. Common student misconceptions:
   - One common misconception is that the digestive system only deals with food intake, when in reality it also plays crucial roles in absorbing nutrients, producing hormones, and protecting the body from harmful substances.
   - Another common mistake is thinking that the stomach is responsible for breaking down food mechanically; instead, this process primarily occurs in the mouth through chewing and the small intestine through enzymes.
