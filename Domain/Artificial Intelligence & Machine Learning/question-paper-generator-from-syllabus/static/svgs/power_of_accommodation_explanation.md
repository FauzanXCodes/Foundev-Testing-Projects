2. Real-world applications:
The Power of Accommodation is a psychological concept that refers to an individual's ability to adjust their behavior or perspective to better fit a particular situation or environment. This concept can be applied in many real-life situations, such as dealing with stress, adapting to change, improving communication skills, and fostering empathy and understanding in interpersonal relationships.

3. Common student misconceptions:
   - Some students may misunderstand the Power of Accommodation as a passive approach or adjusting one's personality to please others. In reality, it involves adapting behaviors flexibly while still maintaining one's personal values and integrity.
   - Another misconception might be that accommodation is only relevant in social situations, whereas it can also apply to academic, professional, and other areas of life.

4. Interactive learning suggestions:
   - Role-playing scenarios: Present students with various real-life or hypothetical situations where they must demonstrate the Power of Accommodation. Encourage them to discuss their thoughts and strategies, and provide feedback on their approaches.
   - Group discussions: Organize group activities where students share experiences related to accommodation and learn from each other's perspectives and solutions. This helps foster empathy and understanding among group members.
   - Interactive quizzes or case studies: Create interactive quizzes, case studies, or simulations that challenge students to apply their knowledge of the Power of Accommodation in various contexts.
   - Reflective journaling: Encourage students to keep a reflective journal where they document instances when they have demonstrated the Power of Accommodation and its impact on themselves and others. This helps students become more aware of their own accommodation abilities and identify areas for improvement.
