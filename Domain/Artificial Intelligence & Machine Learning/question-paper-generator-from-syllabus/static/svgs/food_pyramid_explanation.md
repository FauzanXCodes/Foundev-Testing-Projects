# Educational Guide
1. Key elements explanation:
   - Background (#f0f8ff): This is the background color of the SVG diagram.
   - Text ("food pyramid"): The title of the diagram, which is centered and readable.
   - Rectangle (100% width and height): The container for the food pyramid diagram itself.
   - Title: Metadata providing information about the diagram's type, topic, and purpose.
   - Description: Additional metadata with a brief description of the diagram.
   - Data-map-type, data-zoom-level, and data-topic: Custom attributes that may be used for web development purposes to enhance user interaction or provide additional information about the diagram.

2. Real-world applications:
   - Nutrition education in schools
   - Health and wellness programs
   - Dietary advice from healthcare professionals
   - Food labels and packaging

3. Common student misconceptions:
   - Misunderstanding serving sizes or portion control
   - Thinking all fats are unhealthy and should be avoided
   - Believing that carbohydrates are bad for you
   - Ignoring the importance of balanced nutrition

4. Interactive learning suggestions:
   - Virtual food pyramid simulations where users can drag and drop different food groups to see how they fit into the recommended dietary balance
   - Quizzes or games that test students' knowledge of serving sizes and healthy food choices
   - Interactive infographics comparing different diets (e.g., vegan, vegetarian, pescatarian, omnivore) to help students understand the importance of balanced nutrition in various dietary lifestyles
   - Virtual cooking classes or meal planning tutorials that incorporate lessons on healthy food choices and portion control
