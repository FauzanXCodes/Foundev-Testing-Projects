2. Real-world applications:
This SVG diagram can be used in educational settings to teach students about different modes of reproduction used by single cells, such as mitosis, meiosis, binary fission, budding, and spore formation. It can also help learners understand key concepts related to cell division, genetics, and developmental biology.

3. Common student misconceptions:
   - Many students struggle with the difference between mitosis and meiosis, as they both involve cell division but lead to different outcomes (mitosis produces two identical cells, while meiosis produces four genetically unique cells).
   - Another common misconception is that all cells undergo both mitosis and meiosis throughout their life cycle. In reality, only certain types of cells undergo these processes, such as somatic cells (which undergo mitosis) and germ cells (which undergo meiosis).
